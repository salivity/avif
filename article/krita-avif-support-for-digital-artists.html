Krita AVIF Support for Digital Artists

This article explores how Krita integrates the AVIF (AV1 Image File Format) to benefit digital painters and concept artists. It covers the technical advantages of the format—including high dynamic range (HDR) compatibility, superior compression efficiency, and wide color gamuts—and explains how these features streamline digital painting workflows, texture preservation, and online portfolio presentation.

Native HDR and Wide Color Gamuts

Krita is one of the few open-source digital painting programs offering end-to-end High Dynamic Range (HDR) painting workflows. AVIF serves as a primary delivery format for this work because it natively supports 10-bit and 12-bit color depths, along with HDR transfer functions like Perceptual Quantizer (PQ) and Hybrid Log-Gamma (HLG). For concept artists designing lighting keys or matte paintings for film and modern game engines, exporting to AVIF ensures that luminance levels and smooth color gradations are displayed accurately on HDR screens without the banding artifacts common to standard 8-bit formats like JPEG.

Advanced Compression with Texture Preservation

AVIF is derived from the AV1 video codec, offering substantially better compression ratios than JPEG, WebP, or PNG. Concept art and digital illustrations frequently feature complex, high-frequency details such as fine brushwork, film grain, and intricate blending. When exporting to lossy AVIF from Krita, artists retain these subtle canvas textures and soft gradients at significantly smaller file sizes. This eliminates the blocky artifacts and chroma subsampling issues that often ruin detailed painted assets.

Full Alpha Channel Support

Unlike traditional JPEG files, AVIF supports full alpha-channel transparency. Digital artists frequently need to export isolated character designs, foliage cutouts, props, and UI components. Krita allows artists to save these transparent assets directly into AVIF, achieving smaller file footprints than PNG without sacrificing edge sharpness or semi-transparent brush strokes.

Streamlined Web and Portfolio Delivery

For artists hosting personal portfolios or sharing work across the web, bandwidth and page load speeds directly impact viewer engagement. Because AVIF is supported by modern web browsers, artists can use Krita’s AVIF export settings to generate lightweight, production-ready images for online galleries. The reduced file weight ensures fast loading times while keeping the visual fidelity identical to the original high-resolution canvas.

Configurable Export Settings in Krita

When saving or exporting artwork as an AVIF in Krita, creators have granular control over their output:

  • Lossless vs. Lossy: Artists can choose lossless compression for archiving master copies or lossy compression for web distribution.
  • Color Depth Control: Users can select between 8-bit, 10-bit, or 12-bit color depths depending on the canvas requirements and the target display hardware.
  • Speed and Quality Sliders: Krita provides controls to balance encoding speed against final file compression, allowing artists to prioritize maximum space savings or rapid iteration during concept drafts.
